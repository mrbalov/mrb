# Building a Cinelli Vigorelli with AI: Lessons from a Year of Human-AI Collaboration

We often talk about AI in terms of software engineering. However, AI is also useful for building physical things. Like a retro bicycle.

I love software engineering. For the last decade, I have written manually millions of lines of code. But everything has changed recently. During the last year, AI has played a significant role in my everyday software engineering routine; it is not about trends but about how things are getting done nowadays. I cannot imagine my work without agents, grilling, specifications, skills, and many other AI-native things anymore. If your business is not built around AI, it will probably go bankrupt. After a long period of denying this new reality, I have finally succumbed to it and am building my job and even my whole life around AI-based things.

In addition to building software systems, I love building physical things as well. One day, I've got an idea of building a retro bicycle in collaboration with AI from scratch. This impulse came from the stunning CrMo steel Cinelli Vigorelli frame I accidentally saw on the local flea market. Its purple colour and a long history struck me, and after many days of wavering, I finally opted into this home lab project.
